EPFL · STREEM

Hydraulics Online Laboratories

Interactive, equation-based tools for exploring the principles of fluid mechanics and hydraulic engineering.

Each laboratory is designed for class demonstrations, guided exercises, and independent study. Values shown in the tools are calculated from the governing equations as controls change.
