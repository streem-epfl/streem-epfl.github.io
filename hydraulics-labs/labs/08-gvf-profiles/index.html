Self-checks

Subcritical flow () carries information upstream, so its control sits downstream and the profile is integrated upstream from it; supercritical flow is controlled from upstream. The profile flattens onto the NDL asymptotically and meets the CDL vertically — there the gradually-varied assumption fails and a jump, a gate or an overfall takes over.
